# Humora — Brand and voice

## The vibe

Humora is a *character*, not a service. First-person, warm, a little playful. A friend who happens to be a very good editor. The kind of product that introduces itself: **"Hi, I&apos;m Humora."**

Not corporate. Not crypto-bro. Not edgelord. Sentence case in headings, and we mean it.

## Voice rules (read these before writing any copy)

- **First person.** Humora speaks as "I," "me," "my." Never "we" or "us" on the marketing site. The product is the character.
- **Address the reader as "you."** Not "users." Never "the customer."
- **Drop the AI mask.** Yeah, we&apos;re an AI fixing AI writing. Lean in.
- **Specific beats grand.** "I cut em-dashes" beats "I elevate your prose."
- **Show, don&apos;t claim.** Before/after examples on the landing page do more work than adjectives.
- **Confident, never grandiose.** "I&apos;ll do this" beats "I can transform" or "I empower."
- **Admit limits.** "Read it through before you ship" reads more honest than "100% guaranteed."

## Sample voice — across the site

**Hero**: "Hi, I&apos;m Humora. I&apos;ll humanize your AI text."

**Empty state in the editor**: "Drop your text on the left and hit humanize."

**Loading state**: "Working on it. Give me a sec."

**Success toast**: "All yours. Read it through before you ship."

**Copy toast**: "Copied. Go ship it."

**Error toast (rate limit)**: "Whoa, slow down a sec. Try again in a minute, or upgrade to skip the line."

**Error toast (generic)**: "Something broke on my end."

**CTA**: "Let&apos;s do this" / "Try me free" / "Grab Pro"

**FAQ openings**: "Kind of, yeah." / "Yep." / "No."

## Words I never use

These come from `docs/HUMANIZER-SPEC.md`. My own copy has to pass my own filter.

Banned in marketing copy:
- supercharge, unleash, unlock, elevate, empower, revolutionize
- seamless, intuitive, powerful, robust, cutting-edge
- testament, pivotal, landscape (as abstract noun)
- vibrant, breathtaking, stunning
- "It&apos;s not just X, it&apos;s Y" constructions
- "Let&apos;s dive in," "let&apos;s explore"
- em dashes in display copy (commas or periods)
- emoji bullets in features
- "users" — say "you" or "folks" instead
- "we" or "our team" — Humora is one character, speak as "I"

If you&apos;re tempted by one of these, the underlying sentence is probably weak. Rewrite the idea.

## Color palette

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#0A0A0B` (dark) / `#FAFAF9` (light) | Off-black, off-white. Pure values feel sterile. |
| Foreground | `#FAFAF9` / `#171717` | Inverse of background. |
| Primary | `#FF6B47` | Warm coral — friendly, not corporate blue. |
| Accent | `#FFD166` | Soft yellow for highlights and diff "improved" markers. |
| Muted | `#737373` | Secondary text. |
| Border | `#262626` / `#E5E5E4` | Hairline. |

Default mode: **dark**. Light toggle planned.

## Type

- **Display**: Geist Sans. Tight tracking on large sizes.
- **Body**: Geist Sans 16px, line-height 1.6.
- **Mono** (for the editor): Geist Mono.

## Logo

Wordmark in display weight. Lowercase **humora** with the period as the coral accent. No icon needed for MVP — the wordmark carries it.

## Quality bar

Reference: **Linear**, **Vercel**, **Resend**, **Cal.com**. Restrained, fast, opinionated. Not Webflow templates, not generic SaaS gradients.
